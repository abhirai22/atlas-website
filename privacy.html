1. Who we are

This website is operated by ATLAS Business Solutions LLP (“ATLAS”, “we”, “us”), a limited liability partnership registered in India. ATLAS delivers its services through four wings — AXIS, ORBIT, COSMOS and ATLAS Platforms — all of which operate under the same legal entity. ATLAS is the data fiduciary for personal data collected through this website.

This policy is written with reference to India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules. It covers this website only. Personal data we handle while delivering services to a client is governed by our agreement with that client.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you request an ATLAS Pulse or otherwise contact us, we collect what you choose to provide:

  • your name, company name and work email address;
  • your phone number, if you provide it;
  • your organisation’s approximate headcount and the service area you are interested in;
  • anything you write in the message field.

Please do not include sensitive personal data — such as health, financial account or identity-document details — in the message field.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, our hosting provider receives technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages requested and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site, keep it secure and diagnose faults.

3. How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange your ATLAS Pulse.
  • To prepare proposals and communicate with you about services you have asked about.
  • To keep records of our business correspondence.
  • To operate, secure and improve this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Why we are allowed to

We process the personal data you submit on the basis of your consent, which you give by submitting the form for the purposes described above. You can withdraw that consent at any time by contacting us (see Contact us); withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out. We may also process personal data where it is required by law or for other legitimate uses permitted under applicable law, such as keeping our website secure.

5. Who we share it with

Your enquiry is shared only with the ATLAS team members who need it to respond. We also use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf and under our instructions:

  • Website hosting and delivery — Cloudflare, which serves this website and protects it against abuse.
  • Web fonts — Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces used on this site. Your browser requests the fonts directly from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • Form handling and email — the service providers we use to receive form submissions and correspond with you.

We may also disclose personal data where required by law, court order or a government authority, or in connection with a merger, restructuring or sale of our business. Some providers may store or process data outside India; where they do, we rely on transfers permitted under Indian law.

6. Cookies and tracking

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not use third-party tracking pixels. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the site against bots and abuse. If we add analytics or similar tools in future, we will update this policy first.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to manage any resulting relationship. If an enquiry does not lead to an engagement, we delete it within 24 months of our last contact with you, unless we must keep it longer to meet a legal obligation. Server logs held by our hosting provider are kept for a short period according to that provider’s own retention settings.

8. How we protect it

This website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we restrict access to enquiry data to the people who need it. We apply reasonable security safeguards to prevent personal data breaches. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; if a breach affecting your personal data occurs, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected or updated;
  • have your data erased when it is no longer needed;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity;
  • raise a grievance with us, and, if it is not resolved, complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before acting on your request.

10. Children

This website is intended for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date at the top of this page shows when it last changed. Where a change is significant, we will take reasonable steps to let you know.
